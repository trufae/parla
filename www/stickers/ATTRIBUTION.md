# Sticker artwork: credits and license status

This document covers the four packs in Parla's starter collection (85 stickers).
An individual pack ZIP contains only the images from that pack. Keep this file
with the images when copying or sharing a download.

Artwork rights remain with their respective rights holders. Parla's software
license does not license these images. Creator credits and Telegram source links
are attribution, not permission to convert or redistribute the artwork.

The credits below were checked on 2026-10-22. Named artists and animators are
credited by the linked catalog; their credit does not establish copyright
ownership. No original copyright notice or license authorizing Parla's conversion
and redistribution was verified for any of these packs. Do not describe them as
public domain, Creative Commons, GPL, or otherwise freely licensed without
supporting evidence.

## Conversion and permission records

Parla's packaging scripts convert the animated Telegram stickers to GIF and
rename the images for Parla's picker. Radare's images remain WebP. These format
changes do not establish ownership or grant an artwork license.

Before treating a pack as cleared for distribution, record the rights holder,
the exact license or written permission covering conversion and redistribution,
its source, any required copyright notice, and the files it covers. Preserve the
original notice and license text when available. For mixed artwork or character
adaptations, verify the relevant underlying rights as well.
